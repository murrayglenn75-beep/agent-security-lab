# Discovery-to-Verification research gate

This small experimental metadata gate is designed to prevent model-generated assertions being labelled verified solely because they look plausible or another model agreed.

States: proposed, reproduced, independently_checked. An independently checked label requires a source version and digest, reproduction and independent-check artifacts, separate checker, distinct method statement and named human reviewer. Status **eligibility** is all that is assessed: the module cannot prove that reviewers are real, hashes correspond to accessible artifacts, the method is genuinely independent, or that the mathematical claim is correct.

Never use this as an autonomous publication authorization system. Any real workflow must validate artifacts and hash bytes at the trust boundary, verify signatures or identities, check independence/conflicts of interest, preserve append-only corrections, and require human judgment. For AAK/Brain-AI/APEX/FORGE/TANDEM/Kestrel use as design reference, not proof of existing runtime integration.

Research trigger: October 2026 discussion around OpenAI's published mathematics manuscripts and the difference between result generation, reproducibility, and broad independent scientific understanding.
